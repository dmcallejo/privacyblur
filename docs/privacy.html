Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

PrivacyBlur Revived does not collect, store on a server, or share your personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your pictures are processed on your device and are never uploaded by the app.

Who is responsible

PrivacyBlur Revived is developed by Diego (dmcallejo on GitHub) as an open source project. Contact: 3715013+dmcallejo@users.noreply.github.com.

Your pictures

You pick a picture from your device with the system picker. Blurring, pixelating, rotating and scaling happen locally in the app. When you save the result, it is written to your device's photo library. The app does not send pictures anywhere. The app keeps working copies of the pictures you open in its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

Permissions

  • Photos and storage: to open a picture and to save the result. On Android, the app uses the system photo picker where available.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Face detection

The "full" version of the app (for example on Google Play) offers automatic face detection. It uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device; your pictures are not uploaded. Google documents that ML Kit may send technical usage and performance metrics to Google. These metrics do not contain your pictures. The "foss" version of the app (for example on F-Droid) does not include ML Kit or any Google component.

Data stored on your device

The app remembers the location of the last picture you opened so that it can offer it again at startup. This is stored only on your device.

Third parties

The app's own code makes no network requests and contains no third-party analytics or advertising. App stores such as Google Play and F-Droid may collect their own data when you download the app, under their own policies.

Your rights

Because the app does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete. You can remove all data the app stores on your device by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. The history is available in the source repository.

PrivacyBlur Revived is a fork of PrivacyBlur by MATHEMA GmbH.
